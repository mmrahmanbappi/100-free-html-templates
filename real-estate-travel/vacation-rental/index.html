About the cabin

Fernhollow is a hand-built log cabin on four acres of woodland, with its own little dock on Lake Laurel. Wake up to mist on the water, paddle out in the canoe, and finish the day in the hot tub under the stars.

Inside there's a stone fireplace, a big farmhouse kitchen and a loft bedroom kids love. The Wi-Fi is fast enough for video calls, though most guests forget about it.

House rules

  • No smoking inside. The fire pit is fine
