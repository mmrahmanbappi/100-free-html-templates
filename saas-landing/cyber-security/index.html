FAQ

Questions

Do small businesses really need cyber security?

Yes. Small businesses are targeted because they usually have weaker protection. Basic steps like two-step login, backups and staff training stop most common attacks.

What is a security operations centre (SOC)?

A SOC is a team of security analysts who watch your systems all day and night, investigate alerts and respond to attacks. With Bastion, you get this without hiring your own team.

How much does managed cyber security cost?

Our packages start at $15 per employee per month. Most businesses with 24/7 monitoring pay $32 per employee per month.

What should I do if I think we've been hacked?

Don't turn devices off or pay any ransom. Disconnect affected devices from the network and call our 24/7 incident line on +1 (800) 555-0199.
