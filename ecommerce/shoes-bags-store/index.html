Help

How do I know my shoe size?

Stand on a piece of paper, mark your heel and longest toe, and measure the distance. Match that length to the foot length column in our size guide.

Can I exchange for a different size?

Yes. Exchanges are free within 45 days. We send the new size as soon as you drop off the return.

How should I clean my sneakers?

Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap. Remove the laces and insoles and let them air dry. Don't put them in the washing machine.
