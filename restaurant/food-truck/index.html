    Questions we get a lot

    Do you take cards?

    Yes. Cards, phone pay and cash. No minimum spend.

    Is anything vegetarian or vegan?

    The charred corn and black bean taco is vegan, and so is the veggie bowl without cheese. Our tortillas are corn, so they're gluten free too.

    What happens if it rains?

    Light rain, we're still there. Heavy rain or strong wind, we post on Instagram by 9am and this page updates too.

    Can we order ahead for a big group at the truck?

    Text us on (503) 555-0147 at least 2 hours ahead for orders over 15 tacos and we'll have it ready.
