Help

How long does pizza delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 25 to 35 minutes. If your pizza takes longer than 45 minutes within our delivery area, your next order is free.

Do you have gluten-free pizza?

Yes. Our gluten-free base is made in a separate area, but our kitchen uses flour, so it's not suitable for people with coeliac disease.

Can I collect my order?

Yes. Choose collection at checkout and your pizza will be ready in about 15 minutes.
