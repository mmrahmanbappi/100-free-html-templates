// faq

Good questions to ask any web agency.

How much does a website cost?

Most small business websites cost between $2,400 and $6,000. Online shops start at $5,900 and custom web apps at $12,000. You get a fixed price in writing before any work starts.

How long does it take to build a website?

Four to six weeks for most business sites, and eight to twelve weeks for online shops and web apps.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes. We build on tools that let you change text, photos and prices without coding, and we give you a recorded training session.

Do I own the website?

Yes. You own the domain, the design, the code and all the content. If you ever leave us, we hand everything over.

Do you offer hosting?

Our care plans include fast hosting, daily backups, security updates and up to two hours of changes each month.

Tell us about your website.

Answer four quick questions and we'll send a fixed quote within one working day. No sales calls unless you ask for one.
