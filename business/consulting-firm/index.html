FAQ

Before you call us.

How much does a consulting project cost?

Most projects cost between $40,000 and $180,000, depending on size and length. After our first call we send a fixed fee in writing, so there are no surprises.

How long does a typical project take?

Between 8 and 20 weeks. Many clients then keep us on a small monthly retainer for a year to keep the plan on track.

Do you work with small businesses?

We work best with companies that have between 30 and 2,000 staff. For smaller firms we run a six-week growth programme at a lower fixed price.

Will you work alongside my team?

Yes. We build the plan with your managers so they own it. We don't write a report and disappear.
