Questions

How much does Fernway cost?

Your first 10 invoices are free. After that we charge a flat 1.5% of each invoice you get paid early. There are no monthly fees.

Does my client need to sign up?

No. Your client only needs to confirm the invoice by email and pay as they normally would. Most never need an account.

Is there a minimum or maximum invoice size?

You can get paid early on invoices from $250 to $250,000.

Which countries do you support?

Instant payouts work in 38 countries, including the US, Canada, the UK and most of Europe. More countries are added every quarter.
