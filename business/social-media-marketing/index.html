Questions we hear a lot

How much does social media management cost?

Our plans start at $790 a month for two platforms and 12 posts. Most local businesses choose our Growth plan at $1,650 a month, which includes reels, a monthly shoot and replying to comments.

How long until I see results?

Most clients see more engagement in the first month and steady follower growth from month two or three. Paid ads can bring in customers within the first week.

Do I have to be on camera?

No. We can build your content around products, your team or your space. If you'd like to appear, we'll coach you so it feels natural.

Will you post without my approval?

Never. You see every post in advance and can approve, change or reject it from your phone.
