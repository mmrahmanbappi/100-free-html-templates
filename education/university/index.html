How to apply

For undergraduate courses starting in September 2027.

  1. Pick your courses

    Use the course finder, come to an open day on 17 October or 21 November, and talk to current students.

  2. Apply online

    Apply through the national admissions service by 28 January 2027. Our course codes are listed above.

  3. Get your offer

    Most decisions arrive within 4 weeks. Some courses, like nursing and art, have an interview first.

  4. Accept and arrive

    Accept your place, apply for your room and join us for Welcome Week in late September.
