      Safe, every single day

      The things we never skip, so you can go to work without worrying.

      Checked staff

      Every adult has an enhanced background check and paediatric first aid training.

      Secure doors

      Keypad entry, and children only go home with adults on your list, with a password.

      Allergy aware

      Nut-free kitchen, colour-coded plates and a written plan for every allergy.

      Photo diary

      See naps, meals and nappy changes on our parent app, plus photos through the day.

      Sleep checks

      Babies are checked every 10 minutes while they nap, and we write it down.

      Inspected

      Rated Outstanding at our last inspection. Read the full report at reception.
