Slow mornings by the sea

A 36-room resort on the Pacific coast of Costa Rica, with two pools, a quiet beach and breakfast served until noon.

Barefoot, but properly looked after

Casa Palmera sits between the jungle and a long, calm beach near Nosara. Rooms open onto gardens or the ocean, the kitchen cooks with fish caught that morning, and there's always a hammock free.

We're 90 minutes from Liberia airport and happy to arrange your transfer, surf lessons or a day of doing nothing at all.

Rooms and suites

Every room has air conditioning, a rain shower, fast Wi-Fi and breakfast included.
