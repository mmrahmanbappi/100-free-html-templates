FAQ

Questions about branding.

How much does branding cost?

A logo and visual identity with us starts at €9,500. A full brand with strategy, packaging and guidelines is usually €22,000. Larger rebrands start at €35,000.

How long does a branding project take?

Six weeks for an identity package and around twelve weeks for a full brand with packaging.

What's the difference between a logo and a brand?

A logo is one part of a brand. A brand also includes your name, colours, typefaces, tone of voice, photography style and packaging, all working together.

Do we own the final designs?

Yes. When the final invoice is paid, you own full rights to the logo and all designs we create for you.
