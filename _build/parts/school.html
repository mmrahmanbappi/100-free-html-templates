    Admissions

    Starting in Reception in September 2027? Here's what to do and when.

    1. Come and look around

      Tours every Tuesday at 9:30am from October. Book online or call the office.

    2. Apply through the council

      Apply on the council website by 15 January 2027. Put us in your list of preferences.

    3. Offer day

      You'll hear from the council on 16 April 2027. Accept your place within two weeks.

    4. Settling in

      Stay and play sessions in June and a home visit from your child's teacher in July.
