Questions

How do I find my ring size?

Measure the inside diameter of a ring that fits you and match it to our size guide, or order our free ring sizer. If the size is wrong, we resize it free within 60 days.

Is your gold solid or plated?

All our gold is solid 9k or 18k gold, and our silver is 925 sterling silver. We never use plating.

Where do your stones come from?

Our pearls, diamonds and gemstones come from certified, conflict-free suppliers, and we can share the source of every stone.
