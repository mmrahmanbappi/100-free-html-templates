Questions

What is the best way to plan your day?

Pick your three most important tasks, block time for them around your meetings and leave some free time for surprises. Stillday does this for you each morning based on your calendar and deadlines.

Does Stillday work with Google Calendar and Outlook?

Yes. It syncs both ways with Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar, so changes show up everywhere.

Is my data private?

Your tasks and notes are encrypted and never sold or shared. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Can I import from another to-do app?

Yes. Import from Todoist, Things, Microsoft To Do or a CSV file in a couple of clicks.
