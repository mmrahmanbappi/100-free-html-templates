Questions

Is Relay really free?

Yes. The Free plan is free forever for teams of up to five people, with unlimited tasks and three projects. No credit card needed.

Can I move my projects from another tool?

Yes. Import from Trello, Asana, Monday or a spreadsheet in a few clicks. Our team will help for free on the Business plan.

How does per-user pricing work?

You pay for each team member with an account. Clients using the portal are always free.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from your settings page. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month and yearly plans can be refunded for unused months.
