Questions patients ask

I'm really nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Yes, and you're not alone. Tell us when you book. We'll go slowly, explain each step, and agree a hand signal so you can stop at any time. Headphones and a weighted blanket are there if you want them.

How much is a first visit without insurance?

A new patient exam with X-rays is $129. If you also want a cleaning the same day, it's $199 in total.

Do you see children?

Yes, from their first tooth. A child's check-up and clean is $75 and we have a small play corner in the waiting room.

What if I have tooth pain on a weekend?

Call our main number. On Saturdays we're open until 1pm, and outside hours the message gives you our on-call dentist's number.
