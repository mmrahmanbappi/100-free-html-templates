Brand new? Start here.

Most of our students had never done yoga before. This is the path we suggest for your first month.

  1. Week 1: Gentle Basics

    Learn the main poses slowly, with lots of time to ask questions. Two classes this week is plenty.

  2. Week 2: Breath and Balance

    Add simple breathing and some standing balance work. Wobbling is part of it.

  3. Week 3: Slow Flow

    Link the poses together at an easy pace. You'll start to feel stronger and looser.

  4. Week 4: Pick your favourites

    Try an all-levels class, then keep going with whatever felt good.
