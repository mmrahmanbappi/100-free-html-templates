Three generations, one oven.

Rosa opened Crumb & Co. with one oven and her mother's recipes. Today her son Daniel bakes through the night, still using the same sourdough starter from 1998.

Everything is made by hand in our kitchen behind the shop. Nothing is frozen, and leftover bread goes to the local food bank every evening.

Questions

How far ahead should I order a cake?

Please order at least 3 days before you need it. For weddings and cakes with more than two tiers, give us 2 weeks.

Do you make gluten-free or vegan bakes?

Yes. We bake gluten-free brownies and vegan banana bread every day, and can make most cakes vegan with 5 days' notice. Our kitchen uses flour and nuts, so we can't promise allergen-free.

Can you deliver?

We deliver cakes within 8 miles of the shop for £6. Bread and pastries are collection only.
