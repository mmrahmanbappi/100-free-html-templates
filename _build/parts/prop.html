About this home

This top-floor flat sits in a Victorian house turned into four homes. Big sash windows face south over the park, so the living room gets sun from late morning until evening.

The open-plan kitchen and living room has oak floors and a working fireplace. Both bedrooms are doubles, and the second one works well as a home office. The owners replaced the kitchen and bathroom in 2024.
