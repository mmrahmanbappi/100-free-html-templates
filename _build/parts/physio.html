    What should I wear?

    Something loose, like shorts and a T-shirt, so we can see and move the sore area.

    Do I need a GP referral?

    No. You can book straight in. If you have a referral or scan results, bring them along.

    Can I claim on my insurance?

    Yes. We have instant claiming for all major health funds and handle work injury and car accident claims.
