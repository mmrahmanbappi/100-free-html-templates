Questions

How does online grocery delivery work?

Pick your products, choose a 1-hour delivery slot and pay online. A local driver brings your shop in cool bags, usually within 2 hours of ordering.

What if an item is out of stock?

You can choose a similar item as a swap, or ask us to leave it out. You're only charged for what arrives.

Is there a minimum order?

The minimum order is £25. Delivery is £2.99, or free when you spend over £50.
